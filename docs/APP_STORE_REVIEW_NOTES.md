## Notes for Review

MixWise is a free home cocktail companion. It helps people browse recipes, track bottles in a home bar, and see what they can make. MixWise does **not** sell alcohol, process alcohol orders, or provide delivery.

**Demo**
- No demo account is required. You can browse recipes and use core features as a guest.
- Optional sign-in: Sign in with Apple, Google, or email + password.
- Magic-link email sign-in is intentionally hidden in the iOS app (those links open Safari and cannot return the session to the app).

**Sign in with Apple**
- Available on the sign-in sheet alongside Google (Guideline 4.8).

**Account deletion (Guideline 5.1.1)**
1. Sign in.
2. Open the **You** tab (or Account).
3. Scroll to **Delete my account**, type DELETE, and confirm.
4. The account and associated user data are removed.

**Content / age**
- The app contains cocktail recipes and home-bar tools related to alcohol.
- Age rating should reflect alcohol-related content (typically 17+).
- We do not sell or facilitate purchase of alcohol.

**Privacy**
- Privacy Policy: https://www.getmixwise.com/privacy
- We collect account email/name, product interaction analytics (PostHog), optional photos when the user shares a pour to Instagram/Facebook Stories, and user content such as bar lists and notes.
- Tracking is off (no ATT / IDFA advertising tracking).

**Permissions you may see**
- Camera / Photos — only when the user chooses to share a pour photo to Stories.
- Notifications — optional local reminders for a daily drink suggestion (not remote push marketing).
- Face ID — optional app lock if the user enables it in settings.

**Sharing**
- Instagram and Facebook Stories sharing uses Meta’s Sharing to Stories APIs when those apps are installed.
- System share / copy link are always available as fallbacks.

Thank you for reviewing MixWise.
